# Apparels Village Limited (AVL) Group - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This FAQ has been compiled directly from the official website knowledge base of Apparels Village Limited (AVL). It is designed to provide quick, direct answers regarding factory specifications, operations, materials, compliance, and client relationships.

---

### 1. What is Apparels Village Limited (AVL)?
Apparels Village Limited (AVL) is a premier 100% export-oriented apparel manufacturer based in Bangladesh, founded in 2002. A private limited company and a major concern of the **Faiyaz Group**, AVL specializes in high-quality knit and woven apparel production, employing over 3,965 professionals.

### 2. Where is the AVL factory located and what is its size?
The production facility is strategically located in Savar, Dhaka, Bangladesh, approximately 17 kilometers from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport. 
* **Site Area**: 3.67 acres of land.
* **Covered Floor Area**: 154,251 sq. ft.
* **Green Area**: 42.9% of the site is dedicated to open green space, supporting employee health and sustainable operations.

### 3. What are AVL's production units and their capacities?
AVL operates two primary apparel manufacturing units, along with support knitting and dyeing divisions:
* **Knit Garments Unit**: Spans 78,500 sq. ft. across five floors, with a monthly production capacity of over **2 million pieces**.
* **Woven Garments Unit**: Spans 114,800 sq. ft. across a six-floor building, with a monthly production capacity of over **1.5 million pieces**.
* **Knitting Unit**: Features high-capacity machinery with a daily fabric production capacity of 10,000 kg.
* **Dyeing Unit**: Equipped with advanced dyeing machines with a daily capacity of 15,000 kg.

### 4. Which certifications and compliance standards does AVL hold?
AVL's facilities strictly adhere to international manufacturing, ethical, and environmental standards, holding the following credentials:
* **Product Standards**: OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (for Woven Recycled Materials and Printing Unit).
* **Organic & Materials**: GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard), OCS (Organic Content Standard).
* **Recycled Content**: GRS (Global Recycled Standard), RCS (Recycled Claim Standard).
* **Social & Ethical Compliance**: BSCI (Business Social Compliance Initiative) and SEDEX.
* **Environmental Practice**: Higg Index alignment and ZDHC (Zero Discharge of Hazardous Chemicals) compliance.

### 5. What kinds of woven fabrics does AVL manufacture?
AVL produces an extensive range of woven fabrics, including:
* **Cotton**: 100% Cotton, 100% Organic Cotton, and BCI (Better Cotton Initiative) Cotton.
* **Blends**: CVC (Chief Value Cotton), Oxford CVC, T/C (Tetron/Cotton), and PC (Polyester Cotton).
* **Synthetics & Special Textiles**: 100% Polyester, Recycled Materials Fabric, Denim, 100% Viscose/Rayon, 100% Linen, Mixed Linen, Poly Viscose, Ripstop Fabric, Cotton Flannel, and Cotton Twill.

### 7. What sustainability practices does AVL follow?
AVL focuses heavily on environmentally friendly garment manufacturing by:
* Dedicating 42.9% of the factory site to green spaces.
* Utilizing modern, energy-efficient production machinery (e.g. Bruckner stenters, Lafer open width compactors).
* Ensuring zero discharge of hazardous chemicals (ZDHC compliance).
* Verifying supply chain traceability using organic and recycled certifications (GOTS, GRS, OCS, RCS).
