# About Us – AVL


H1: About Us

H2: About Us

H2: Home / About Us

H2: The Story of AVL

H2: A Vision of Excellence: The Birth of Apparels Village Limited
In 2002, driven by a vision to revolutionize Bangladesh’s RMG sector, the founding members of Faiyaz Group launched Apparels Village Limited (AVL), under the leadership of Md. Monir Hossain.

H2: From Humble Beginnings to Major Upgrades
By 2006, significant enhancements were made, increasing the production capacity from three garment lines and minimal machinery to twenty garment lines, eight circular knitting machines, and four flat knitting machines.

H2: A Commitment to Innovation and Compliance
Since 2012, AVL has honed its focus on HR and operations while continually upgrading internal systems. Their dedication led to notable achievements, including certifications such as Standard 100 by OEKO-TEX® and social compliance audits by BSCI & SEDEX.

H2: Achieving New Heights in 2024
In 2023, AVL inaugurated a state-of-the-art 6-story inventory building and integrated advanced machinery in their dyeing and finishing lines. By 2024, AVL stands as one of the top apparel manufacturers.

H2: Why Choose AVL

H2: Streamlined Logistics Management
Our progressive clearing and forwarding management system prioritizes resourceful goods handling and expedited customs clearing. By reducing lead times and fast-tracking our supply chain, we ensure timely delivery and maintain a competitive edge.

H2: Comprehensive Quality Assurance
Our dedicated quality inspection team performs meticulous self-inspections on behalf of our clients, upholding stringent quality standards. We are committed to aligning with buyer specifications to deliver exceptional products consistently and punctually.

H2: Strategic Raw Material Sourcing
We source raw materials from trusted and experienced producers, leveraging our strong relationships with reputable partners. This strategic sourcing ensures high-quality inputs, enhancing our production capabilities and reinforcing our commitment to excellence.

H2: Sustainable Production Practices
At AVL, we are dedicated to minimizing our environmental footprint through sustainable production processes. Our commitment to green practices includes energy-saving technologies, wastewater management, and the use of eco-friendly raw materials.

H2: Principles

H2: Innovative Foundations: The Principles Driving Our Success

H2: Regular Capital Reinvestment
Regular capital reinvestment is a cornerstone of our management policy, driving continuous improvement and innovation. Our commitment to reinvesting in state-of-the-art technology has led to the establishment of a cutting-edge woven unit, with a capacity of 18,000 pieces of woven garments per day.

H2: Creative Atmosphere
From our modest beginnings in the 2000s, we have embraced self-reliance, integrity, and excellence, guided by the principles of Organizational Culture and Innovation Management. This framework fosters a creative and productive atmosphere, enabling us to meet and exceed customer expectations.

H2: Bounded by Responsibility
We are dedicated to producing high-quality garments in a work-friendly environment, supported by our BSCI and SEDEX certifications. Our commitment to ethical and sustainable practices, including advanced safety systems and rigorous quality standards.

H2: Export-Oriented Focus
As an export-oriented company, AVL specializes in serving clients across Europe and the Americas (North and South). We proudly partner with prominent buyers such as C&A, Oviesse, AS Color, Aldi, NKD, Ferrari, Teddy Smith, and US Polo Assn, among others, delivering exceptional products tailored to diverse international markets.

H2: Thorough and Comprehensive
With over 23 years in the RMG sector, we’ve perfected our ability to meet complex requirements with precision. Our comprehensive approach ensures meticulous attention to design, fabric quality, dyeing accuracy, and dimensional standards. By adhering to industry norms and rigorous quality control, we deliver seamless, high-quality results.

H2: Industrial Expansion
The RMG sector has undergone significant transformation over the past 50 years, fueled by advancements in machinery, engineering and policies. To stay ahead, we employ cutting-edge technologies and strategic frameworks such as Lean Manufacturing and the Supply Chain Operations Reference (SCOR) model.

H2: Safety and Environment

H5: Ensuring Safety with Advanced Systems
To ensure a safer working environment for our workers and employees, AVL has made significant efforts by introducing advanced fire safety measures, regular drill systems, robust building safety features, and intelligent ventilation mechanisms adhering to NFPA standards. As a result, we have successfully attained NOC for building safety and ACCORD Remediation.

H2: Environment

H5: Ensuring Safety with Advanced Systems
We hold three fire licenses that comprehensively cover our production, utility, and administrative areas.
Environmental Stewardship and Waste ManagementAVL is deeply aware of the environmental impact of fabric production and takes critical steps to minimize it. We strictly adhere to local environmental conservation laws and international waste management standards.
To meet effluent discharge requirements, we’ve installed a cutting-edge effluent treatment plant with a daily capacity of 2,700 m³. Powered by Membrane Bio Reactor (MBR) technology from Japan’s Toray, this plant efficiently removes suspended solids without the need for clarifiers or sedimentation tanks.
Resource Conservation and Sustainable PracticesOur commitment to sustainability extends to resource conservation. We’ve recently added a reverse-osmosis-based recycling plant with a capacity of 25 m³ per hour. This system ensures water quality for reuse in production, significantly reducing groundwater extraction daily, underscoring our dedication to a more sustainable future.

H2: Safety

H5: Comprehensive Workplace Safety and Support Systems
At AVL, we recognize the importance of equipping our employees with the skills necessary for multifunctional roles, ensuring they can deliver optimal results in any situation.
Safety TrainingsWe emphasize rigorous training programs to elevate the expertise of our workforce. Our comprehensive sessions include First Aid, Fire Safety, PPE usage, Machine Safety, and pandemic protocols. Specialized training for mid-level management ensures adept handling of processes, while extensive pandemic training reinforces health guidelines and emergency responses.
Childcare CentreTo support new parents, AVL provides an all-in-one childcare center, managed by skilled supervisors. This facility offers a secure and stimulating environment, enabling employees to concentrate on their work while knowing their children are well cared for.
Health CareWe prioritize health with first aid boxes and responders on every floor, along with three MBBS doctors and two paramedics for routine check-ups and treatment. Partnerships with local medical centers enhance our health support infrastructure, ensuring comprehensive care for our workforce.
Fire SafetyAVL’s robust fire safety framework includes monthly drills and a night fire program every 90 days. With over 800 workers trained in firefighting, UL-listed fire doors, PA systems, and a Central Fire Detection System (CFDS) to NFPA standards, our facility is equipped with fire hoses, extinguishers, and protective gear for optimal safety.
Evacuation ProceduresDetailed evacuation plans are prominently displayed on every floor, with monthly drills conducted to ensure all employees are proficient in emergency procedures. This ensures a coordinated and swift response during emergencies.
Air VentilationHeavy-duty exhaust fans and hygienic air circulation systems are installed on each floor. We meticulously monitor and control temperature and ventilation to ensure a safe and comfortable working environment.
Cleanliness and HygieneWe uphold stringent hygiene standards, maintaining dry and clean production units. Our facility provides pure drinking water and fully equipped washrooms, ensuring the highest levels of cleanliness and sanitation throughout.
